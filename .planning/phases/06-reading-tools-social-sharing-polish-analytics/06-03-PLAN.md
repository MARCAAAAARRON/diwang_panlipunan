# Plan 06-03: Internal Platform Analytics Dashboard

<threat_model>
## Security Threat Model (ASVS Level 1)

| Threat | ASVS Requirement | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| PII / User Tracking Data Leakage | V13.1.1 (Privacy by Design) | The platform strictly adheres to zero-PII tracking. View counters and search term aggregations store zero IP addresses, zero device fingerprints, and zero user identifiers. |
| Unauthorized Analytics Access | V4.1.1 (Access Control) | Route `/admin/analytics` is protected by Next.js session middleware and Supabase RLS policies ensuring only `role === 'admin'` can access analytics metrics. |
| Insecure Counter Manipulation / Flood | V11.1.4 (Business Logic Limits) | Page view tracking employs lightweight deduplication and debouncing to prevent flood manipulation. |
</threat_model>

<user_story>
**As an** editorial administrator or researcher,  
**I want** to monitor platform-wide readership metrics, popular articles, thematic distribution, and frequent search queries,  
**so that** our editorial board can identify gaps in public education, allocate research resources, and assess the impact of our civic curriculum.
</user_story>
