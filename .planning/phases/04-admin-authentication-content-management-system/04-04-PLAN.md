# Plan 04-04: Citations Management & Academic Evidentiary Curation

<threat_model>
## Security Threat Model (ASVS Level 1)

| Threat | ASVS Requirement | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Malicious Citation URL Schemes (`javascript:`, `data:`) | V5.1.3 (Input Sanitation) | Citation URLs are strictly validated to require `http://` or `https://` schemas before database mutation. |
| Insecure Reordering / Out-of-bounds Indexes | V5.1.5 (Data Validation) | `order_index` sequence is normalized server-side in a single atomic transaction or sequential update to prevent index collisions. |
| Orphaned Citation Records | V4.1.2 (Access Control) | Citation mutations strictly enforce foreign key integrity against verified `subtopic_id`. |
</threat_model>

<user_story>
**As an** editor or academic researcher on the editorial board,  
**I want** to manage academic citations attached to subtopics, add new references, and reorder bibliography items directly on the article edit screen,  
**so that** all published factual assertions remain transparently cited and verifiable.
</user_story>
